This course reviews relevant material in linear algebra aimed at
first year students of the
[Master of Data Science](https://www.uni.lu/fstm-en/study-programs/master-in-data-science/)
program at the University of Luxembourg.

## Reviewed Topics

- Vector Spaces, Linear Maps, Matrix Representation, Rank-Nullity theorem
- Eigenvalue Problems, Diagonalization, Jordan Decomposition
- Inner Product Spaces, QR Decomposition, Least Squares Method, Spectral Theorem
- Normed Vector Spaces, Matrix Norms
- Singular Values Decomposition, Principal Component Analysis
