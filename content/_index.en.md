### Research Interest
My research is in **discrete differential geometry (DDG)**, which aims to identify
structure-preserving discretizations of the smooth theory. Specifically, I study
**discrete conformal equivalence** theories, **circle packings/patterns**, and
their relationship to the **rigidity theory** of polyhedra, **hyperbolic geometry**,
and **low-dimensional topology**.

A recurring theme in my research is the use of methods amenable to numerical
implementation, paving the way for mathematical experiments and interdisciplinary
collaboration.
